Privacy Policy – TinyWeather
Last updated: February 15, 2026

1. Introduction
TinyWeather is a weather application that displays weather information and city images. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

2. Data We Collect
TinyWeather does not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require you to create an account or provide any personal information.

3. Location Data
TinyWeather does not access or use your device's location services. Weather information is displayed based on cities you manually search for within the app.

4. Network Requests
The app connects to the internet solely to retrieve weather data and city images for display purposes. No personal data is transmitted during these requests.

5. Widgets
TinyWeather offers home screen widgets that display weather information for your last visited city. This data is stored locally on your device using SharedPreferences and is never transmitted to external servers.

6. Third-Party Services
TinyWeather does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Data Storage
All app data (last visited city, cached images) is stored locally on your device. No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud services.

8. Children's Privacy
TinyWeather does not knowingly collect any data from children or any other users.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact
If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at: contact@tinyweather.town
